Dispatcher checklist

  • Before dispatching, call the customer to confirm they are still with the vehicle. 3 GOA in 2 days is the entire completion gap.
  • Assign the job to the driver who is actually rolling, not the owner login. Agero only reads GPS from the assigned driver's phone.
  • Quote ETAs from the driver's real position. Long South Indy and Columbus runs blew 90-minute ETAs by 20 to 40 minutes.
  • Close every job with a callback and tell the customer a one-minute survey is coming. Your NPS base is 4 responses.
  • Never let an offer expire. If you can't take it, reject with a reason inside the window.

Owner items not a dispatcher fix

  • Location sharing. Confirm with Dispatch Anywhere support that the Agero location feed is fully enabled, drivers run every job from the DA mobile app with location set to Allow Always, and each driver has their own login.
  • Photo quality. Agero's check wants four pickup corners, VIN, plate and pre-existing damage before the drop-off set. Today only drop-off photos reach Swoop.
  • GOA vs cancel. Parked. Job 113856465 shows Agero texted the customer, got "service not needed", and still finalized GOA. Rule stays out of the checklist until settled.

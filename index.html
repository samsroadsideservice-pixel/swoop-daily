Dispatcher checklist

  • The 15-minute rule (Agero, Sep 9). The only outcome that does not count against completion is a customer cancel within 15 minutes of acceptance. GOA counts. Unsuccessful counts, even a dead starter on a jump. So read the notes the moment we accept: if it says "needs a key made," wrong service, or anything we can't finish, call the customer right away and have them cancel with Agero and reorder the right service (locksmith, tow).
  • Never cancel after accepting. A reassign (we accepted, then cancelled) counts against completion and acceptance and pays nothing. If we cannot run it, reject the offer inside the window instead. 3 reassigns and 4 GOA in the 45-day window are the completion gap.
  • Before dispatching, call the customer to confirm they are still with the vehicle.
  • Assign the job to the driver who is actually rolling, not the owner login. Agero only reads GPS from the assigned driver's phone.
  • Quote ETAs from the driver's real position. Runs over 30 miles are where the ETA misses are.
  • Close every job with a callback and tell the customer a one-minute survey is coming. NPS base is 30 responses.
  • Never let an offer expire. If you can't take it, reject with a reason inside the window.

Owner items not a dispatcher fix

  • Location sharing. Agero says it is a proximity bonus, not a penalty (Sheila, Sep 9). Post-dispatch sharing reads 88%; pre-dispatch is the gap. Known issue with third-party dispatch platforms; Agero is working on it and sent a driver setup job aid. Drivers keep the DA app sharing for the whole shift, each on their own login.
  • Scoring is per rate agreement (Sheila, Sep 9): volume and cost decisions look at each agreement on its own. Fort Wayne's collapse never touched Indy. Reopening Fort Wayne or adding North Indy zips (Lafayette, Kokomo, Marion were in the old 60-mile radius) cannot hurt the Indy scores. Email Sheila to add zips; pink zips on Swoop's Territory Map are 30-day opportunity, darker = more volume.
  • Photo quality. Agero's check wants four pickup corners, VIN, plate and pre-existing damage before the drop-off set. Today only drop-off photos reach Swoop.
  • South Indy reach. The South agreement is pulling Columbus and Martinsville runs at 30 to 42 miles. Every ETA miss so far is one of those. Worth a look at the zip list on that agreement.
  • GOA vs cancel, resolved. Customer cancel inside 15 minutes is the only free outcome. GOA and Unsuccessful both count. Sheila agrees unsuccessful jumps (bad starter, alternator) are outside our control and has raised it with Agero leadership, but today it counts.
